## Recordings (the sound icon's sheet of notes, 2026-11-12)

site/assets/sound/notes-N.mp3 (docs/sound.md) is cut from two sample libraries, both CC0 (public domain dedication),
which ask for credit and get it here and in docs/sound.md:

- VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (Versilian Studios; VSCO-2-CE on GitHub): the cello, viola and
  violin sections, the clarinet, the horn, the solo contrabass, the tuba and the harp.
- The Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL on GitHub): the grand piano.

Only their recorded notes are used, shortened, filtered and mixed into one file by tools/sound/sheet.py. Their own
organ set is not used: its notes file does not plainly state CC0.
