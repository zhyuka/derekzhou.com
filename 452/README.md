# #452 cumulus life: crops

Seed 1, laptop 1440x900, one free cumulus aimed at from 2.5 km, 4 shots 20 s apart (60 s); base (main) left, head right.
Made with tools/clouds/crops.mjs (claude/crops-stepvp: --stepvp 160x100, each shot rendered at 1440x900).

- crop.png, crop.1.png, crop.2.png, crop.3.png: the full frame at 0, 20, 40, 60 s.
- top4x.png: the aimed cumulus' top, 4x, rows 0/20/40/60 s, base | head.

The head's tower stands taller (its swell, never below the baked body) and settles about 17 px against the base's own
drift over the 60 s (about 6% of its height, the steepest rate the 12-30 min life allows), with no step between shots
and no change in its density. The foreground ground differs between arms by the last shot (a rock loaded in one arm,
not the other): land detail streams on wall time, not on the change.
