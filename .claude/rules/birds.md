# The ink birds (src/birds/)

Read docs/birds.md first.

- The ink birds, drawn in the world, are the only birds: no 2D bird script, SVG or CSS (no-2d-birds).
- Nothing masks a bird for the words, and no bird fades in time (no-masking-boxes, no-fades). No bird is held against
  its ground either: that hold never ran and is gone (the owner, 2026-11-05); every bird's light scale (aM.y) is 1.
- One bird system (the owner, 2026-11-06): every bird is a living-sky bird (210, lvMake); perching on a letter or the
  pointer is a state a bird passes through (270), never a type. A clicked bird is an ordinary bird from its first
  frame: no field or branch may record how a bird was born; a click decides only where and how it enters, and it
  counts with every flock (LV_POP + G_CLICK_OVER).
- The pointer: read by the world's two passive hover listeners (hoverSync) and stored only; the birds react in
  their own step; nothing else on the page reacts to it.
- src/birds/model/ is served as written (the build copies it); facetform.js and model.js are generated by
  tools/birds/ (python3 -I): change the generator and its data, never the output by hand; CI makes them again.
- Research data must be MIT/BSD/Apache/ISC/zlib/Unlicense/CC0, or CC BY with credit (licences).
- npm run check:quick after each edit.
